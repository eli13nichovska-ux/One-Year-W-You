DEAR ELENA, 02/03

For you,

Some things are better written down.

A year ago, I didn't know how many places your name would become attached to. Now, every city, every road trip, every random stop along the way feels connected to you somehow.

This isn't really a collection of destinations. It's a collection of moments. The places matter, but they're not the reason I remember them. I remember them because you were there.

Maybe that's what this is about. Not where we went, but the fact that, for a while now, I've had someone I want beside me for all the places still left to see.

Always with love, Eli ♡
