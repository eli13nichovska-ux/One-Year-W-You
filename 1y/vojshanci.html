THE MEMORY

It wasn't really
about Vojshanci.

We went to Vojšanci for the First of May, and while it wasn't a grand vacation, it became one of those simple days that I look back on fondly. Martina and I spent part of the day making barbecue, trying our best to get everything right. The food turned out great, although you never really trusted the chicken. It's one of those little moments that still makes me laugh when I think about it.

I have to admit, I wasn't the biggest fan of all the cameras around. Sometimes it felt like there was always a lens pointed somewhere when all I wanted was to relax and enjoy the day. Still, there were plenty of things that made up for it. I especially loved the movie we watched together, and it became one of my favorite memories from the trip. And then there was Spy. If we're being honest, I absolutely crushed everyone.

Looking back, Vojšanci wasn't about doing anything extraordinary. It was about spending a day together, sharing good food, laughing at the little things, and making memories that stayed with me long after we left.
