THE MEMORY

It wasn't really
about Kopaonik.

Kopaonik was only a one-day trip, but it turned into one of those memories I'll always smile about. We left Skopje at 5 in the morning, tired but excited for the adventure ahead.

As soon as we arrived, the first thing we did was find somewhere to eat. That's where we tried Medovača, a honey-based rakija that was surprisingly good. We also spent some time thinking about renting snowmobiles, but we weren't sure if we'd have enough time to enjoy them, so we decided against it.

One thing I definitely couldn't get enough of was the alpine coaster. I went on it several times because it was just that fun. One of those rides was with you, and you recorded the whole thing. Every time I see that video, it brings me right back to that moment.
