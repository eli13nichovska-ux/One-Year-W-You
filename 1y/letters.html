EVERYTHING SINCE 02

One year
of you.

A year sounds like a long time until you try to remember all of it.

The ordinary days, the ridiculous ones, the conversations that lasted way too long, the places we went, the things we laughed about, and all the little moments I probably didn't realize I would remember.

Somehow, all of those things became a year. And somehow, that year became one of the most important parts of my life.
